Rental Agreement

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made between the property owner and the tenant. By accepting this agreement, the tenant agrees to the following terms and conditions:

  • The tenant shall pay the rent on time.
  • No illegal activities shall be conducted on the premises.
  • Property must be maintained in good condition.
  • The tenant is responsible for any damages caused.
  • Failure to comply may result in termination of the agreement.

Please read the agreement carefully before proceeding.
